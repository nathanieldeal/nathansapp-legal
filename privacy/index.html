NathansApp Privacy Policy

Effective September 18, 2026

NathansApp is a personal app for logging fluid intake, urine output, medications, lab values and symptoms, and for keeping a caregiver informed. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you provide

  • Account information: your email address and password (used to sign in; passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider), your name, phone number, profile photo, time zone and role (for example, caregiver).
  • Settings: preferences such as your daily drink goal, drink presets and measurement units.
  • Health logs you enter: drinks (type, amount and time), urine output (amount, color and time), medications (name, dose and time), lab values (such as sodium, potassium, calcium, chloride and glucose), and symptoms (type, severity and notes).
  • Caregiver and dependent information: if you are a caregiver, the profiles you create for people in your care (name, date of birth, phone number, photo and settings) and the link between your account and theirs. Caregivers are responsible for having permission to enter information about the people they care for.

Information collected automatically

  • Push notification token: if you allow notifications, the app stores a device token so caregiver alerts can be delivered to your phone.
  • In-app notifications: the notification messages sent between caregivers and dependents are stored so they can be shown in the app.

NathansApp does not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, NathansApp asks for permission to read your water intake. That data is:

  • used only to show your water intake next to the drinks you log in the app;
  • read and displayed on your device only, and not uploaded to our servers;
  • never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or marketing.

NathansApp does not write data to Apple Health. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices → NathansApp.

How your information is used

  • To provide the app: storing and displaying your logs, charts and history.
  • To notify a linked caregiver when you log an entry, if caregiver notifications are set up.
  • To maintain and secure your account.

Your information is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • You, when signed in to your account.
  • Caregivers you are linked with, who receive notifications about entries you log.
  • Service providers that run the app on our behalf, only as needed to provide it:
    • Supabase for account sign-in and data storage;
    • Expo and Apple Push Notification service for delivering push notifications, which include the notification text and your device token.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information is kept for as long as your account exists. You can delete individual log entries in the app at any time. To delete your account and all associated data, email nathanielwdeal@gmail.com from the address on your account, and it will be removed within 30 days.

Children

NathansApp is not directed at children under 13, and children should not create their own accounts. A caregiver may record information about a dependent, including a minor, and is responsible for that information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes, such as sending Apple Health data to our servers, will be announced in the app before they take effect.
